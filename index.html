Signal from the Stars

A message from a faraway planet was coming in when the station's antenna broke. Scan the star map for parts, answer questions together, and fix the signal before the window closes.

Teachers only. You'll need the teacher PIN, and you'll get a room code to share.

How to play

  1. Take turns tapping a dark tile on the star map to scan it.
  2. Every scan asks a question, and everyone answers it.
  3. Station parts you find repair the antenna and decode more of the alien message.
  4. Repair every part before the questions run out to hear the whole message!
